# Code of Conduct

DotNetDevMCP is a small project and everyone is welcome here: first-time contributors, people reporting their first bug,
and people who just want to ask how something works. This page says how we treat each other.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most confusion is a missing sentence in the docs, not a bad question.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in their second language.
- Say thanks. Reviews, bug reports and reproductions are all real work.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, wiki) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer, [@csa7mdm](https://github.com/csa7mdm), through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/csa7mdm/DotNetDevMCP/security/advisories/new) (it reaches only the maintainer)
or by email to the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer will review every report and respond with what they consider appropriate: a private warning, removal of
content, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
